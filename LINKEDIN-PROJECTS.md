A copy-paste-ready guide for adding Anna's side projects to LinkedIn.

## How to add them

Use **both** of these:

1. **Projects section** — `Add section → Recommended → Add projects`. Purpose-built:
   supports a title, description, dates, a live link, and "contributors." Keeps side
   projects visually distinct from employment. Add every project below here.
2. **(Optional) Umbrella Experience entry** — a single `Founder & Solo Engineer ·
   Self-employed · Jul 2021 – Present` role, with each app as a bullet. Recruiters read
   Experience first, so this pushes the projects higher on the page. Draft included at
   the bottom.

**Framing principle:** lead with *what was built and the stack*, not user counts. The
traffic is early-stage; the engineering + product story is the stronger sell on LinkedIn.
