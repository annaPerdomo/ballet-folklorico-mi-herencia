About Us

Nuestra historia — Our story

Ballet Folklórico MH is a community-rooted Mexican folk dance group based in Hacienda Heights, California. We are a family — made up of dancers of all ages and experience levels who share a passion for celebrating Mexican culture through the joy of Folklórico. From our youngest performers to our adult ensemble, every dancer is part of something meaningful.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to keeping Mexican folk dance traditions alive in Southern California — educating, inspiring, and connecting our community through authentic, heartfelt performance. Whether on a festival stage or at a private celebration, we bring the spirit and pride of México to every audience.

Where We Perform

We proudly perform throughout the greater Los Angeles area, the San Gabriel Valley, and Orange County — including cities such as Whittier, Montebello, Costa Mesa, and beyond.
