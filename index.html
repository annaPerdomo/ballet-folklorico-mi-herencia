About Us

More than a dance group.

Ballet Folklórico Mi Herencia is a community-based Mexican folk dance group made up of dancers of all ages — from age 6 through adults. Some of us have been performing for years. Others are just learning their first steps. Everyone has a place here.

We've performed at county fairs, corporate events, weddings, quinceañeras, theme parks, media events, and school assemblies across Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County. We travel throughout Southern California — no stage is too big, no event too small.

Our Instructors

A family tradition.

Founded and led by husband-and-wife managers Jorge & Francisca Diaz and instructors Angel and Mayra Ramirez, Mi Herencia is a true family affair. Jorge announces each performance, while Francisca works behind the scenes ensuring every costume fits just right. Angel & Mayra’s three children — Tatiana, Natalia, and Sebastian — dance right alongside the rest of the group, celebrating the beauty of Mexican culture one zapateado at a time.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire a ballet folklórico group in Los Angeles?
Pricing depends on the type of event, the length of the performance, and the number of dancers. We perform at festivals, quinceañeras, weddings, corporate events, and schools. Contact us for a free, personalized quote.
What areas do you travel to for performances?
We're based in Hacienda Heights, CA and travel throughout Southern California, including Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County.
Do you perform at quinceañeras and weddings?
Yes! We specialize in quinceañeras, weddings, cultural festivals, corporate events, school assemblies, and private celebrations.
Where are you located?
We're a community Mexican folk dance group based in Hacienda Heights, California.
Do you offer folklórico classes? How do I join?
Yes. We offer folklórico dance classes for children (ages 6–12), teens (13–17), and adults (18+) in Hacienda Heights. The first 4 classes are free. Message us to enroll.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks ahead, especially for peak-season dates. For last-minute dates, reach out — we'll do our best to accommodate you.
