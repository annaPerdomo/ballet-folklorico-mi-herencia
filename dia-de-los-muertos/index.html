Día de los Muertos Folklórico Dancers for Los Angeles & OC

City and park festivals, church carnivals, and companies across Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County book Ballet Folklórico Mi Herencia to mark Día de los Muertos. We bring live traditional Mexican folk dance, in full colorful costume.

A Season of Remembrance

Who books us for Día de los Muertos.

Festivals & Parks

City and county Día de los Muertos festivals, from main-stage sets to family activity days.

Churches & Community

Harvest festivals and carnivals that pair folklorico with the season's traditions.

Companies & Shopping Centers

Office celebrations, shopping center events, and family altars and parties.

We've danced for Día de los Muertos at Heritage Park in Santa Fe Springs, Bella Terra in Huntington Beach, and the St. John Vianney Church harvest festival in Hacienda Heights, and this year we're dancing at Hollywood Forever Cemetery in Los Angeles.

The Performance

What to expect on the day.

  • 15–30 minute sets: often one or two short sets, with 3–8 dancers depending on your event.
  • Dances from across Mexico: each dance is performed by the dancers who know it, so the program depends on who's available on your date. Tell us your favorites and we'll confirm what we'll perform once the lineup is set.
  • You provide a flat 12x12 ft space: plus a power outlet for our speaker or the use of your own sound system, and a changing area.
  • We travel across Southern California: Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County.
  • Book 6–8 weeks ahead: this is one of our busiest seasons, though we'll always try for a last-minute date.
  • Pricing depends on your date and set length: see our pricing guide for ranges.

Common Questions

Día de los Muertos booking questions.

How far in advance should we book for Día de los Muertos?
Día de los Muertos is one of our busiest seasons, so we recommend booking 6–8 weeks ahead. For last-minute dates, reach out anyway; we'll do our best to accommodate you.
Can you perform outdoors, like at a park or cemetery event?
Yes. We perform outdoors and indoors. We just need a flat space roughly 12x12 feet and, if we're using our speaker, a nearby power outlet.
Can you dance to a live mariachi band or our DJ's music?
Yes. We regularly coordinate with a live mariachi band, a DJ, or your event's own sound system. Just let us know when you book.
Can you include a short educational intro about Día de los Muertos traditions?
Yes. We can open with a brief, family-friendly explanation of the holiday's traditions and the dances before we perform, a nice touch for a mixed audience.
How many dancers perform and how long is the show?
Most bookings run 15–30 minutes with 3–8 dancers, often as one or two short sets. Tell us about your event and we'll recommend a lineup.
When are you available?
Evenings and weekends year-round, plus weekday evenings for company events. Our dancers work and study during the day, so weekday daytime performances (including school-hour assemblies) are rarely possible. If your event is on a weekday evening or a weekend, ask.
Can we request a specific dance?
Tell us what you'd love to see. Each dance is performed by the dancers who know it, so the program depends on who's available on your date and we can't promise a specific dance ahead of time. Once the lineup is set, we'll let you know what we'll perform.
