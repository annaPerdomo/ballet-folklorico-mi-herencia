A Quinceañera Tradition

Why families add folklorico to the celebration.

A quinceañera honors where a young woman comes from as much as who she's becoming. A live folklorico performance puts that heritage on the dance floor — the music, the colors, and the dances her grandparents grew up with, performed feet away from her guests.

Families book us as a surprise for the birthday girl, as dinner entertainment between courses, or as a cultural showcase before the night's dancing begins. However it fits your timeline, we build the set around your celebration — and we're known for being flexible and easy to work with, even when the schedule shifts on the day.

The Performance

What to expect on the day.

  • Traditional dances from across Mexico — from the swirling ribboned skirts of Jalisco to the elegance of Veracruz and the fast-stomping zapateado of the north. Every celebration gets its own mix of regions.
  • Song requests welcome — is there a dance or song that means something to your family? Tell us when you book and we'll do our best to work it into the set.
  • Authentic, colorful costumes — the swirling ribboned dresses and charro looks that make every phone in the room come out.
  • A set built around your reception — during dinner, between courses, or as the lead-in to the surprise dance. You tell us the moment; we make it land.
  • Interactive guest moments — we can invite your guests onto the floor for a fun group dance that gets all generations moving.
  • We come to you — banquet halls, event centers, backyards, and church halls across Southern California.

Our Repertoire

Dances from every corner of Mexico.

Jalisco

The heart of folklorico — the iconic ribboned dresses, charro suits, and the sones everyone in the room knows.

Veracruz

Elegant white lace, fans, and graceful jarocho footwork.

Sinaloa

Playful, high-energy banda rhythms that pull the whole room in.

Michoacán

Purépecha traditions full of character, charm, and humor.

Chihuahua & the North

Fast-stomping norteño polkas — pure zapateado.

Chiapas & Baja

Regional gems from every corner of the republic.

The exact lineup for your date depends on the dancers performing that day — so tell us your favorites when you book, and we'll do our best to accommodate your requests.

Common Questions

Quinceañera booking questions.

How much does a folklorico performance for a quinceañera cost?
Pricing depends on your date, venue location, the length of the performance, and the number of dancers. Send us your details and we'll put together a free, personalized quote — no obligation.
How long do you perform at a quinceañera?
We tailor the set to your reception timeline — tell us what you have in mind (during dinner, between courses, before the surprise dance) and we'll build the performance around it.
Do you travel to my venue?
Yes. We're based in Hacienda Heights, CA and travel throughout Southern California, including Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County.
Can our guests dance with you?
Yes! Interactive dance moments are one of the things our clients love most — we can invite guests to join us and get the whole room on its feet.
Can we request specific songs or dances?
We love requests! The exact program depends on the dancers performing on your date, so we can't lock in a specific song ahead of time — but tell us what you'd love to see and we'll do our best to accommodate it.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 3–4 weeks ahead, and earlier for peak-season Saturdays. For last-minute dates, reach out anyway — we'll do our best to accommodate you.
