Folklorico Dance Classes for Every Generation

Weekly ballet folklórico classes in Hacienda Heights for kids, teens, and adults — learn zapateado, faldeo, and traditional dances from Jalisco, Veracruz, Sinaloa, and beyond. Whether you're stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or returning to your roots, Mi Herencia is your home. No experience needed, and your first 4 sessions are free.

First 4 Sessions Free

Try folklorico on us.

The hardest part of any dance class is walking in the door — so we make it easy. Every new dancer's first 4 sessions are completely free. Come see how a practice feels, meet the group, and decide if it's for you. No costume, no commitment, no experience required.

Folklorico is more than exercise. It's the music your family grew up with, the stories of Mexico's regions told through movement, and a community that spans generations — kids, parents, and grandparents dancing side by side. Many of our dancers came in never having danced a step; today they perform on stages across Southern California.

What You'll Learn

Real technique, real traditions — at your pace.

  • Zapateado & footwork — the rhythmic stomping at the heart of folklorico, built up step by step from your very first class.
  • Faldeo & skirt work — the sweeping skirt technique that makes the ribboned dresses of Jalisco come alive.
  • Dances from across Mexico — traditional choreography from Jalisco, Veracruz, Sinaloa, Baja, Chiapas, and more, along with the culture behind each region.
  • Confidence & community — rhythm, posture, and stage presence in a welcoming group where every generation dances together.
  • Performance opportunities — dancers who want the stage can perform with us at fairs, festivals, and community celebrations across Southern California.
Find Your Class

A class for every age.

Youth (Ages 5–12)

A fun introduction to folklórico dance. Children learn basic footwork, rhythm, and the joy of traditional music through movement and play.

Teen (Ages 13–17)

Students develop technical skills in zapateado, faldeo, and regional choreography while building confidence and connecting with their culture.

Adult (18+)

For dancers of all levels who want to connect with their roots. Learn traditional dances from Jalisco, Veracruz, Sinaloa, Baja, Chiapas, and more.

Common Questions

New dancer questions.

Do I need dance experience to join?
Not at all. Our classes welcome complete beginners alongside seasoned performers — youth, teen, and adult groups each meet you at your level, and the first steps are taught from zero.
How much do folklorico classes cost?
Your first 4 sessions are completely free, so you can try classes with no commitment. After that, send us a message and we'll share current rates for your age group.
When and where are classes held?
Classes meet on Tuesdays in Hacienda Heights, CA — an easy drive from Whittier, La Puente, West Covina, and the rest of the San Gabriel Valley. Message us and we'll send the current schedule for your age group and personally invite you to an upcoming practice.
What should I wear to my first class?
Comfortable clothes you can move in and closed-toe shoes are all you need to start. As you continue, we'll let you know about folklorico shoes and practice skirts.
Do students get to perform?
Yes — dancers who want to perform join us on stage at fairs, festivals, and community celebrations across Southern California once they're ready. Performing is always optional, never required.
