Planning Hispanic Heritage Month?

Hispanic Heritage Month is our busiest season — and the dates schools want (especially the weeks of September 15 through October 15) go first. If you're an activities coordinator, PTA volunteer, or teacher planning this year's celebration, summer and early fall are the time to lock in your assembly.

A folklorico assembly turns Hispanic Heritage Month from a bulletin board into an experience: students see the dances their classmates' families grew up with, hear where each one comes from, and — the part they remember — learn steps themselves.

The Program

More than a performance — a lesson they'll remember.

  • Traditional dances in authentic costumes — a colorful tour through the regions of Mexico, from the ribboned skirts of Jalisco to the white lace of Veracruz and the fast zapateado of the north. If your school has a favorite dance or theme, tell us — we'll do our best to include it.
  • The story behind each dance — a short, age-appropriate history of the region and traditions behind what students are watching.
  • Interactive dance lesson — students learn real steps and dance along; teachers have been known to join in.
  • Flexible formats — full-school assemblies, grade-level sessions, or festival performances; we adjust to your bell schedule.
  • In person or virtual — we travel to schools across Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County, and offer virtual programs anywhere.

Common Questions

School booking questions.

What does a folklorico school assembly include?
Traditional Mexican dances performed in authentic regional costumes, a short history of each dance and the region it comes from, and an interactive lesson where students learn steps themselves. We tailor the program to your schedule and age group.
When should we book for Hispanic Heritage Month?
Hispanic Heritage Month runs September 15 – October 15 and those weeks fill up first. Reach out in summer or early fall to lock in your date; for other dates we recommend at least 3–4 weeks of lead time.
What ages and grade levels do you perform for?
We perform for all ages — from Head Start and preschool programs through elementary, middle, and high schools, as well as universities. The narration and interactive portions are adjusted to the audience.
Do you offer virtual performances for schools?
Yes. We've performed virtual programs for school festivals and company events, including dances followed by an interactive lesson your students can follow from their classrooms.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Hacienda Heights, CA and travel to schools across Los Angeles, the San Gabriel Valley, the Inland Empire, and Orange County. Virtual assemblies are available anywhere.
